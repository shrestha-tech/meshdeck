What you can do from the computer

Each feature has its own switch on the phone, per computer, and starts off.

Phone notifications

Every notification on one list, with app names, links you can click, quick replies and dismiss on the phone.

Calls

See who is calling and answer, decline, end or dial from the computer. The call sound stays on the phone.

Call log and dial pad

Recent calls grouped by day with missed-call filter, contact names and a dial pad.

Contacts

Search, and add, edit or delete contacts with e-mails, labelled numbers, address, birthday and notes.

Text messages

Read your SMS conversations and send texts from the computer.

Photos, videos and music

Browse by album, preview and download originals.

Files, both ways

Drag files either direction with a progress panel, resumable transfers and checks for files that already exist.

Private by design

Pair once with a matching code. Encrypted with TLS 1.3 between your two devices. The phone stays hidden until you choose to show it.

On Windows, notifications, call alerts, texts, photos and files work today. The call log, dial pad and contact editing are on the Mac first and come to Windows next.

Pair in a minute

  1. Install MeshDeck on your phone and on your computer, and connect both to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On the computer, click Make visible for 2 minutes.
  3. On the phone, tap Pair next to your computer.
  4. Check that both screens show the same code, then confirm on both.
  5. On the phone, switch on what this computer may use: notifications, calls, contacts and so on.

Not on the same Wi-Fi? Put both devices on a private network such as Tailscale and enter the computer's address and port in the phone's "Not on this Wi-Fi?" box.

Questions

Does MeshDeck need an account or the internet?

No. Your phone and computer talk directly over your local network. There is no account, no cloud service and no telemetry.

Is it safe?

Each device has its own key. You pair by checking that both screens show the same code, and everything is encrypted with TLS 1.3 between the two paired devices. Every feature has its own switch per computer, off by default.

Does the call sound play on my computer?

No. You can answer, decline, end or dial from the computer, but the call audio stays on the phone.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

This preview is not signed with a paid developer certificate. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway. You can check the download against the SHA-256 checksums on the release page.

Which systems are supported?

The phone app needs Android 10 or later. The computer app runs on macOS 13 or later and Windows 10 or 11. An Ubuntu package is planned.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

MeshDeck is built so that your information stays between your own devices. This page explains what that means in practice.

What MeshDeck collects

Nothing is sent to us. MeshDeck has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not receive your messages, contacts, call history, photos, files, notifications or any other data from the apps.

What the apps do with your data

  • The phone app shares only what you switch on, separately for each paired computer. Every feature starts off.
  • Data travels directly between your phone and your computer on your own network, encrypted with TLS 1.3. Each side only talks to the device you paired by checking a matching code.
  • The computer app shows notifications, the call log, contacts and text messages when you ask for them. They are kept in memory while the app runs and are not saved on the computer. Files you choose to copy are saved in your Downloads folder.
  • If you add or edit a contact from the computer, the change is written to the contacts stored on your phone.
  • Pairing keys are kept in the Android keystore on the phone and in the Keychain on a Mac. On Windows and Linux the pairing key is kept as a file in your user profile, protected only by your account's file permissions.
  • The apps do not write message text, notification text, file names or contact details to logs.

Android permissions

The phone app asks for permissions only when you switch the matching feature on: text messages (SMS), contacts (read and write), phone state, call log, answering and placing calls, notification access, photos, videos and music, and shared folders. If you refuse a permission, that feature stays off.

This website and downloads

This site is static: it has no cookies, no scripts, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the downloads are hosted on GitHub Releases. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit or download, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive that information.

Using MeshDeck away from home

If you connect your devices over a private network such as Tailscale, that service's own privacy terms apply to the connection. MeshDeck itself still sends nothing to us.

Children

MeshDeck is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/shrestha-tech/meshdeck/issues.
